# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Zintriq community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Expected**

- Assume good intent; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Credit HAL packs and diagnostics as seriously as code PRs.
- Disclose security issues privately first when disclosure could harm users.
- Keep RFCs and reviews about technical merit and evidence.

**Unacceptable**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or doxxing.
- Publishing malicious extensions or packs, or circumventing the capability sandbox.
- Impersonating another publisher or reusing a removed extension name during cooldown.
- Spamming issues/RFCs or derailing Hardware RFCs without board/HAL evidence.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers listed in the repository. Maintainers may warn, restrict contribution privileges, or ban accounts. Enforcement decisions affecting Hardware RFC reviewers will be noted publicly without private details.
